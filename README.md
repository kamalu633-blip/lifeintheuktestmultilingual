PRIVACY POLICY Life in the UK Multilingual Last updated: May 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Life in the UK — Citizenship Test app ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

The App is operated by Kamal Uddin (contact: kamalu633@gmail.com).

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide: We do not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the App.

Information collected automatically:

Usage data (collected by third-party services):

Google AdMob — If you have not purchased "Remove Ads", the App displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) and general usage data to serve relevant ads. On iOS, the App will ask for your permission before accessing your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). You can opt out at any time in your device settings.
RevenueCat — The App uses RevenueCat to process in-app purchases. RevenueCat may collect anonymous purchase and transaction data to manage your entitlements (e.g. whether you have purchased "Remove Ads").
Data stored locally on your device:

Your study progress (which questions you have answered)
Bookmarked questions
Your quiz history
Your selected language preference
This data is stored only on your device using AsyncStorage and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

Display relevant advertisements (via Google AdMob) to users who have not purchased "Remove Ads"
Process and verify in-app purchases (via RevenueCat)
Improve the App's content and performance
4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other apps/websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by:

On iOS: Going to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and disabling "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
On Android: Going to Settings → Google → Ads and enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"
For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not collect or store your payment information. RevenueCat is used to verify and manage purchase entitlements. Please refer to Apple's and Google's privacy policies for information about how they handle payment data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only with:

Google AdMob — for advertising purposes (see Section 4)
RevenueCat — for purchase management
Apple / Google — for App Store and payment processing
7. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us at kamalu633@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Data Retention

All study progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the App. We do not retain personal data on our servers.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

Access the personal data we hold about you
Request deletion of your personal data
Opt out of personalised advertising
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at kamalu633@gmail.com.
